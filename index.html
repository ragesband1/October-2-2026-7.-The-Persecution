The Persecution

By Harassechon Fudjiwik
In the palace of Vidotsche, King Herodjner's arrogance and greed turn against the warriors of Hesitoch. The enchanted god Persecuria confronts the king and reveals the cost of persecuting the very people whose trust sustains his rule. Explore the palace grounds, help its people, and decide whether the king can change.

Moral: Do not persecute the people who serve your organization. Oppression destroys trust and may be unjust and unlawful.
